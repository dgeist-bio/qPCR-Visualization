qPCR-Visualization 🧬📊

A Python-based tool for the professional visualization of qPCR data, originally developed to analyze gene expression patterns during my Master’s thesis in Biochemistry (completed in 2024) at the University of Ulm.

## ⚠️ Status: In Progress / Migration

This project is currently being migrated to a new MacBook (M5) environment. I developed this automation tool during my final months at GBA Pharma to streamline data workflows.

## Features

- Thesis-Driven Logic: Designed for the relative quantification ($\Delta\Delta C_q$) of leukemia research data.
- Publication-Ready Graphics: Generates bar charts with integrated standard deviations (SD) for scientific accuracy.
- Optimized Aesthetics: Features the Viridis color palette for high contrast, neurodivergent-friendly design, and full accessibility.
  
- Professional Background: Master of Science currently working as a Laboratory Technician in Analytical Services, specializing in HPLC-based assays and method validation parameters (LOD, LOQ, Precision, Accuracy, etc.) according to customer specifications
- Chromatography & Assays: Extensive experience with HPLC (Chromeleon), Assay, Purity, and Identification testing.
- Strategic Planning: Sequence design and process optimization for customer-driven analytical orders.

## Roadmap[ ]
- Generalization: Moving from manual Excel exports to direct raw data processing.
